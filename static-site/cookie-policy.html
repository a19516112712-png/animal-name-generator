🍪 Cookie Policy

Understanding how and why we use cookies on Animal Name Generator.

Last updated: June 3, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the website owners. Cookies may be set by the website you are visiting ("first-party cookies") or by third-party services used by that website ("third-party cookies").

Cookies can be classified by their lifespan: "session cookies" are temporary and are deleted when you close your browser, while "persistent cookies" remain on your device for a set period or until you manually delete them. Cookies can also be classified by their purpose: essential cookies, functional cookies, analytics cookies, and advertising cookies.

2. How We Use Cookies

Animal Name Generator uses cookies for several purposes, all aimed at providing you with a better browsing experience and supporting the operation of our free service. Below is a detailed breakdown of each category of cookies we use.

3. First-Party Cookies

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the Site to function properly. They enable core functionality such as page navigation and access to secure areas of the website. The Site cannot function properly without these cookies.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration

These cookies do not require your consent under applicable privacy laws as they are essential for the Site to function.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We use services from third-party providers that may set cookies on your device when you visit our Site. These third-party providers have their own privacy and cookie policies. We do not control these third-party cookies, and they are subject to the respective privacy policies of the providers.

5. Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to collect information about how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics sets cookies to track visitor behavior and provides us with aggregated reports on page views, session duration, bounce rates, and traffic sources.

7. Cookie Management

7.1 Browser Settings

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies, to accept only certain types of cookies, or to alert you when a cookie is being set.

Instructions for managing cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies
  • Opera: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies

7.2 Opting Out of Analytics

To prevent Google Analytics from collecting data about your visit, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. This add-on is available for all major browsers and prevents Google Analytics JavaScript from sharing information with Google Analytics about your visit activity.

7.3 Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

To opt out of personalized advertising from Google, visit Google Ads Settings. To opt out of personalized advertising from many third-party ad networks at once, visit:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) — US
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA) — EU
  • Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada (DAAC)

Please note that opting out of personalized advertising does not mean you will no longer see ads. It means the ads you see will be less relevant to your interests because they will not be based on your browsing history.

7.4 Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. Our Site respects DNT signals where technically feasible. When we detect a DNT signal from your browser, we disable analytics and advertising tracking cookies accordingly.

8. Consequences of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies, please be aware that some features of the Site may not function as intended. Essential site features such as navigation and content display will continue to work, but analytics and personalized advertising features will be limited. Disabling cookies does not affect your ability to browse and use our name generator tools.

9. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our cookie practices, the services we use, or legal requirements. When we make changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Cookie Policy periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.

10. More Information

For more information about how we handle your data, please see our Privacy Policy. If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, please contact us at support@animalnamegen.com or through our Contact page.
