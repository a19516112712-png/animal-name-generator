📧 Contact Us

We would love to hear from you. Questions, suggestions, or just a friendly hello — drop us a message!

Support Information

📬 Email Us Directly

If you prefer to reach us outside the form, you can email us at: support@animalnamegen.com

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 48 hours during weekdays. Response times may be longer on weekends and holidays.

Why Contact Us?

We welcome messages on a wide range of topics. Here are the most common reasons people reach out and how we can help:

  • Suggest a New Animal: Is there an animal missing from our generator? Let us know and we will prioritize adding it. Include any name ideas you have in mind.
  • Share a Name Idea: Have a brilliant animal name? Submit it and we may feature it in our collections with credit.
  • Report a Technical Issue: Broken links, formatting problems, or any site issue — tell us what went wrong and we will fix it promptly.
  • Partnership Inquiries: Interested in collaborating? We are open to cross-promotions, content partnerships, and community initiatives.
  • General Feedback: Ideas for new features, design improvements, or anything else — we genuinely want to hear from you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Animal Name Generator really free?

Yes — 100% free, no registration required, no hidden fees. We support the site through advertising while keeping all naming tools accessible to everyone.

How long does it take to get a response?

We respond to most inquiries within 48 hours on weekdays. Weekend and holiday responses may take slightly longer. We read every message we receive.

Can I request a specific animal or name category?

Absolutely! User requests are our primary source for expanding the generator. Use the form above or email us directly with your suggestion.

Do you offer advertising or sponsorship opportunities?

We are open to discussing advertising and sponsorship. Please use the Partnership Inquiry option in the subject dropdown and we will get back to you with details.

How do I report a problem with the site?

Select "Report a Technical Issue" in the subject dropdown and provide as much detail as possible — which page, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Our Response Promise

When you contact Animal Name Generator, here is what you can expect from us:

  • Acknowledgement: You will receive an automated confirmation that your message was received.
  • Human Response: A real person will read and respond to your message — not an automated bot.
  • Action: If your message requires action on our part (fixing a bug, adding an animal, etc.), we will let you know the expected timeline.
  • Respect: All communications are treated with confidentiality and respect. We never share your contact information without permission.

Thank you for taking the time to reach out. Your input helps make Animal Name Generator better for everyone, and we truly appreciate every message we receive.
